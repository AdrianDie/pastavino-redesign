About Pasta Vino

A warm, informal meeting place

A warm, informal meeting place where fresh pasta, quality ingredients and authentic Italian flavours take centre stage. Here you get homemade dishes made with love, inspired by traditional Italian kitchens.

At Pasta Vino it is about more than just food. We want to create a relaxed atmosphere where friends, families and neighbours can gather around good flavours and pleasant moments. From creamy carbonara and fresh tagliatelle to classic Italian meat dishes and homemade desserts, we make the food fresh every day with carefully selected ingredients.
